Into the nature  
Between the rocks  
A river

Sometimes we stop everything  
We rearrange, remove and add  
Just for the matter of aesthetics

What is beauty?  
Does it matter?  
Everything matters and don't at the same time

What is my desire?  
Stop with questions  
Chill, observe  
Feel and breathe

Forever changing  
For ever and never

Just shooting words as if  
They meant anything  
And maybe they do

What, how and when?  
Again, no more questions  
Look, listen to the music  
To the birds
