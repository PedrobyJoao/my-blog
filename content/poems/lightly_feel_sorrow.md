Lightly  
Feel sorrow,  
Be sad about your friends,  
Cry about about the most irrelevant things.

But all lightly.  
There is no time or place  
To not feel the positive lightness  
Of existing with other people.

Appreciate the conflicts,  
Lightly.  
Enjoy the sorrow,  
Lightly.

That is the stream of life  
That you may not avoid:  
joy, sorrow, pain, happiness...  
So make sure to feel all lightly

However, deep and present  
Because one day, for certain  
Lightly, your consciousness will turn off

So feel now, lightly.
