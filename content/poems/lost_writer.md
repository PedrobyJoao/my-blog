Wondering with no directions  
Beautiful water-rock battle  
Or better, love

There is no tutorial that could give me the skills  
Poems are either bad or good  
I don't know what the writing is about  
Just write, I do

It doesn't rhyme  
Because no effort, I want to  
But I keep passing my pen

No meaning, like my life  
Oh damn, what a drama  
Yeah, drama...  
What is the meaning?

To where am I heading?  
Should I really rationalize these things  
Or should I just ape around, eating bananas

It might be not the most beautiful  
Not the ugliest  
But still a poem  
And maybe that is life

Life is this  
The awareness of reality

Waves are still sending soldiers  
Against the solid things

Don't they get tired of the same war  
Everyday, the same clash  
Time is tough to catch it

It passes but it's quite unique  
As some would say:  
There is no past or future, these do not exist  
Only the preset

Apes need to eat bananas or something
