The following collection of poems tell us a love story.

Some were written by the man, and some by the woman. Let's
call them Linda and Tulio. The writter will be in the title of each poem.

No more details shall be given :)

<h1 align="center">If we kiss (Tulio)</h1>

So close, we get so close dancing  
I close my eyes  
And I feel the harmony of our bodies

We were just learning, practicing  
But the heat of our movements  
Started to generate something  
A connection wired by our touching

I feel silly sometimes  
Because maybe I'm feeling this alone  
But it doesn't matter, I'm feeling anyway

I love your big expressive eyes  
Fitting your nice open smile  
What if?

What if we also touched the rest of our body?  
There is only one part left:  
My lips in yours  
Just after our best dance

<h1 align="center">We almost kissed (Tulio)</h1>

It was not just me  
You were also feeling the same  
Dang! Such a good emotion  
What are we really feeling?

Is it worth the time and effort?  
Hum...  
It's not. I tell you, darling  
It's not worth to question it!

Come on, free yourself  
Come closer with your body and soul  
I want to feel all of you  
And make you touch all of me

Don't worry, hug me  
Step inside into my arms  
Look inside at my soul  
That is what it matters

I really like to look into your eyes  
As long as it's possible  
I really wish  
We could fully enjoy this moment together

Please, free yourself darling  
Freely feel whatever your soul demands  
Let's live each second  
Without thinking on the ones to come

Sweet  
Simply sweet as a good brigadeiro  
Made with love  
That is how I describe you

I'm not a sugar person  
But I want to taste all of your nutrients  
I want to get high in glicose from you

Even if that seems risky  
There is nothing to be concerned

Baby smell, look into my eyes  
Don't think, just feel the moment  
Feel this wonderful feeling between the two of us  
Come, kiss me now

<h1 align="center">We didn't kiss (Tulio)</h1>

Broken heart, she got me  
How is that even possible?  
Having known her only for 12 days

Anyway, I cried  
Such a sad feeling  
But wonderful at the same time

Why and how I got so vulnerable?  
It seems I was ready  
To fully dive into her arms

We had such a lovely night the day before  
I was teling her how I felt  
And she was doing the same

She looked into my eyes and said:  
"I can see your soul through your eyes"  
She would come into my arms and hug me

She also was ready to dive into me  
It was just a matter of days  
At least I thought

Wow, what a lovely night  
Part of me still hopes she is coming back  
Like right now looking for me

But I guess I should just accept  
If she wants to wait until it's the last one  
Anyway...

I cried twice thinking about her  
And she doesn't seem to care much  
Yes, she seems a bit down  
But for other reasons

Not a poem anymore  
Just records of lovely and sad days  
Of when I though I found one  
One that I could fully connect

But no. She felt the same but no  
That is why I cried  
Because the connection existed  
We simply, or she, decided to..  
Make it like never happened

She has her reasons  
I don't blame her  
I just express my sadness  
In this notebook iluminated by the moonlight

Beautiful sky  
Clouds, stars and a lightning moon  
This kind of night reminds me  
Of our dancing in the dark sessions

Darling, it was more than beautiful  
Way more than beautiful  
Now, it's all done  
Even though you're still here

It was..  
Enough, there is no language  
That can explain how special we were

Now, it's time for me to finish this chapter  
I don't want to keep thinking in vain  
Finito is finito

It may be hard for me  
Probably not for you  
But I'm withdrawing from you  
One day of lovesickness was enough

I'll get back to my peaceful solitute now  
Or maybe that is just me saying  
But in reality, I'll still find difficult  
To even look at your neutral expressive eyes

Did you really could easily forget us?  
I mean, we're living in the same place  
When you look in the depth of my eyes  
Don't you see it? Our..  
Our love?

Oh my, I'm just a fool  
I'm done thinking about what happened  
But that is ok  
I'm alive and I'll live fully

I'll dig everything and everyone with  
All my soul. Afterall..  
I'm close to my own death

<h1 align="center">We are kissing (Tulio)</h1>

Under the morning sunlight  
Something was bothering my internal peace  
I had a broken heart  
I was trying to respect your decision

But I couldn't hold myself  
I had to express what my soul urged for  
How much I missed you that one day

The one day that you were there  
You looked into my eyes  
But we ere not seeing each others souls anymore

I then expressed  
You got me surprised  
You had a tough and sad day too  
You were missing me

Rive, we went there as friends  
Suddenly, we were hugged upon a rock  
Watching the water flowing  
The birds singing  
And feeling

In the same day  
Under the warm of my blanket  
We shyly kissed but barely  
My heart was beating up too fast  
So as yours

All that was just...  
Wait, so wonderful:  
Flying, searching for lovers  
Kissing every one of them  
Yellow, fluffy

Now, anyway.. We're kissing  
Kissing  
We both still have worries but we're here

I feel I have to leave you  
I'm diving too deep into your soul  
And I have to be careful  
Not drowning myself in your love

To be honest, I don't think you feel the same  
I know you like me  
But for you a lot of things about us is wrong  
I got it. You're trying to protect yourself

About my feelings:  
I made the mistake of thinking  
Of trying to analyze what we have  
Instead of just let it be

I don't know what I'm feeling  
I know that I like you  
But there is an invisible wall between us  
Made by our thoughts and fears

I bet that behind the wall, my dear  
Or better without it  
We would be the purest lover primates  
Holding each other tight

But that is a fiction  
The wall is not leaving  
Anyway, forget everything that I said

Don't make the same mistake as me  
Just live, enjoy the moment  
The us

I'll try to do the same, darling  
While we're still here
