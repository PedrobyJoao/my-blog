Walking through somewhere  
Not confused  
Through dharma my mind flourishes  
My body and mind, the fusion

I'll run away  
Or maybe I'll look and learn  
But I preferred instead to stay  
Oh my, oh my, all I did was not in vain

The day is beautiful  
Orange star shining towards us  
Conscious groupments of atoms and whatnot  
It calls them and me to enjoy its warmth

Hey mamma star, I'm going  
I'll just fulfill my belly with stardust  
With energy I'll be flowing
