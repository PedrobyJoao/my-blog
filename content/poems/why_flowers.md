Flowers, why flowers?  
Why not trees  
But sometimes, we talk about trees

Walking around the beautiful  
Awareness of my brain  
Focused on the breath  
But also on the pain

Standing still  
I still can feel my brain still  
Calm, slowly moving out

Oh brain, I know you like it  
Oh brain, thanks for this  
Oh brain, oh Brian!
